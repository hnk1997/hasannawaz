About me

I design for the moment someone hesitates before completing a booking or a payment.

7 years in product design, most of it spent on high traffic consumer platforms where conversion and trust decide whether someone follows through. I’ve redesigned core booking flows that cut drop-off by 19%, validated through A/B testing and held across every season, not a one-time spike. I’ve shipped features focused specifically on payment trust and reducing transaction anxiety at checkout, work that’s given me a real interest in fintech and the psychology of money moments.

That same thinking now carries into enterprise design work, digital experiences & bilingual design systems built for institutions operating at serious scale and stakes. That work has been recognised at Cannes Lions, the industry’s most prestigious award, for a digital experience I helped shape from the ground up.

I use AI throughout my design process to accelerate prototyping, simulate workflows, uncover edge cases, synthesize analytics, and evaluate product risks. I believe AI delivers the greatest value when paired with strong product thinking, systems design, and human-centered decision-making.

When I’m not designing, I enjoy traveling and hiking in the mountains to recharge and gain fresh perspective. I’m also into strategic gaming, which keeps me engaged with complex systems, variables, and quick decision-making.
